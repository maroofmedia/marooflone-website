The cave has attracted archaeological interest, with researchers reportedly discovering stone tools in the area believed to date back to around 6000 BC. According to local folklore, the cave extends beneath the mountains and opens somewhere into Wular Lake, although this underground connection remains unverified. A fascinating site where Kashmir's prehistoric past meets local mystery."
