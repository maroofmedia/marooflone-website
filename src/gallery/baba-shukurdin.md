    A panoramic view of the Wular Lake landscape, photographed en route to Rampur Rajpora, North Kashmir. The hilltop is home to Baba Shukur Din Ziyarat, a well-known spiritual site. To the left lies Bangladesh village in Bandipora, also known as Zurimanz, while Watlab in Baramulla stretches across the right.

    Interestingly, Bangladesh gets its name from a local story dating back to 1971. According to local accounts, after a fire destroyed several houses in neighbouring Zurimanz, families settled on nearby land and built new homes. Around the same time, East Pakistan became the independent country of Bangladesh, and the new settlement came to be known by the same name. It was officially recognised as a separate village in 2010.
